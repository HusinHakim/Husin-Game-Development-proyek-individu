Aberrant

A roguelite where the arena is your only weapon.

About

You are a slime that escaped a cult ritual gone wrong. You can't punch, swing, or shoot — the only way to fight back is to grab whatever the environment gives you and throw it at your enemies.

Pick up chains, crystal bucklers, and stalagmites scattered around each room. Aim with your mouse. Land hits, dodge attacks, and survive long enough to finish the cult's final guardian.

Features

  • No-attack combat. All damage comes from environmental items you pick up and throw.
  • Iron Chain. Wraps enemies on hit, locking them in place for follow-ups.
  • Crystal Buckler. A timed parry shield. Block at the right moment to stun the boss.
  • Stalagmite. Heavy ranged throwable.
  • Ritual gating. The boss room stays sealed until you read three altar books.
  • Multi-phase boss. The Corrupted Golem has melee, ranged, and a one-shot AoE ult at 50% HP.
  • Adaptive audio. Positional ambience around the boss; theme music fades on entering the arena and returns when the fight starts.

Controls

WASD Move
Shift Dash (brief invincibility, costs stamina)
Left Click Throw active item toward cursor
Right Click Raise shield (parry window)
1–5 / Wheel Switch inventory slot
G Drop active item
E Read ritual book
F Interact (portals, doors)
